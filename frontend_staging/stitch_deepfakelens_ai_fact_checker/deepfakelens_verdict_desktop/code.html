Forensic Analysis

Scientific consensus, supported by extensive geological and astronomical data collected over decades (including physical samples returned from the Apollo missions), definitively proves that the Moon is composed of silicate rock and metals, not cheese.

The composition of the lunar crust consists primarily of oxygen, silicon, magnesium, iron, calcium, and aluminum. The "green cheese" notion is a historical myth and metaphorical expression originating from the Middle Ages.
